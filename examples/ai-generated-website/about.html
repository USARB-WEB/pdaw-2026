How something gets on the list

Four steps, in this order. A product that fails one of them does not reach the shelf, however good the margin looks.

  1. 1 · We buy it ourselves

    No demo units from distributors. We pay retail for the first one so the supplier has no reason to send us a good sample.

  2. 2 · Someone uses it for a month

    Daily work, not a lab bench. Most problems only show up in week three.

  3. 3 · We check what a repair costs

    If a screen replacement costs half the price of the device, we do not stock it.

  4. 4 · We write down who it is for

    Every product page says what the thing is good at. If we cannot finish that sentence, it does not go on the list.
